**Topic:** career · **Difficulty:** strategy

## Story
You have strong infra security (endpoint, cloud, network, data). The high-paying
jump is into **Product Security / Application Security / DevSecOps**, where your
background plus demonstrable app+pipeline skill is rare and valuable.

## What the ₹70+ LPA band expects
- **AppSec depth:** OWASP Top 10 and API Top 10 — exploit and fix, not just name.
- **Threat modeling** and **secure code review** (interviews test both live).
- **DevSecOps:** SAST, DAST, SCA, secret scanning wired into CI with a gate.
- **Cloud-native:** IaC, container, and Kubernetes security (your cloud edge).
- **Supply chain:** SBOM, signing, provenance.
- **Communication:** explain risk to engineers and leadership.

## Your proof
These labs and rooms are a public **portfolio** you can show and talk through —
that is what separates candidates at this level. Rebuild one fix from memory,
and be ready to screen-share and explain any of it.

## What you'll do

interview stories (problem → what you did → outcome).
